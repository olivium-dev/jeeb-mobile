## What the captures DID prove — the two error states are indistinguishable

`password_strength_error` and `password_mismatch_error` are separate frozen
identifiers, separate `liveRegion` nodes, and separate cubit verdicts — but both
render the same string, `setpwValidationError`:

> "Passwords must match and meet the strength requirements."

The two re-captured states are **byte-identical PNGs**:

```
cmp docs/redesign-midnight/captures/M3-26/…__2-strength-error.png \
    docs/redesign-midnight/captures/M3-26/…__3-mismatch-error.png
→ identical
```

So a user who mistypes the confirm field and a user who chooses a weak password
are shown the same sentence, which names both faults and tells them neither.
The screen's own source has flagged this since JM-061 (`50_ROUTE_REQUESTS.md`
requested dedicated keys and they were never written), but nothing measured the
user-visible consequence until the Midnight captures made the two states
comparable frame to frame.

| Key | EN | AR | Site |
|---|---|---|---|
| `passwordStrengthError` | `Choose a stronger password — at least 8 characters, with a letter and a number.` | `اختر كلمة مرور أقوى — 8 أحرف على الأقل، مع حرف ورقم.` | `password_security_screen.dart` `password_strength_error` note — today renders `setpwValidationError`. |
| `passwordMismatchError` | `Those passwords don't match.` | `كلمتا المرور غير متطابقتين.` | `password_security_screen.dart` `password_mismatch_error` note — today renders `setpwValidationError`. |

The AR strings above are proposals, not translations of record — the l10n lane
owns the final wording. The EN strength copy must stay consistent with whatever
`ChangePasswordPolicy` actually enforces
(`lib/features/password_security/domain/change_password_policy.dart`).

Two further keys are wanted but are **not** blocking and are not proposed here,
because the current field reuses the login labels and reads correctly:
`passwordCurrentLabel` / `passwordCurrentHint` (today `loginPasswordLabel` /
`loginPasswordHint`).
