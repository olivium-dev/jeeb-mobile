## 1. The two R11 tile literals

The board's map leg names the DROP-OFF explicitly and uses sentence case. The
shipped keys are generic and Title Case (doc-13 Pattern F).

| key | EN (tile literal) | AR | replaces |
|---|---|---|---|
| `captureLocationDropOffCallout` | `Drop-off here` | `مكان التسليم هنا` | `captureLocationPinCallout` ("Pin here") |
| `captureLocationConfirmDropOffCta` | `Confirm drop-off` | `تأكيد مكان التسليم` | `captureLocationPinCta` ("Pin Location") |

`@` for the callout: "Label on the glass pill above the fixed centre pin on the
capture-location map. Sentence case, one line, ellipsized."
`@` for the CTA: "Primary CTA on the capture-location sheet. Sentence case."

**Blocked on**: `captureLocationPinCta` is pinned by
`find.text('Pin Location')` in `test/delivery_create_screens_test.dart:254` and
`test/capture_location_map_injection_test.dart`. Whoever lands the ARB change
must update both finders in the same commit.
